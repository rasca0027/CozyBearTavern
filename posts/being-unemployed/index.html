Unemployment Diary #2

June 24, 2026 Kelly Chang

I thought being unemployed would be fun, like I finally have time to do everything that I wanted to do for so long. But in reality, I was sitting in front of my desk all day, everyday, prepating for interviews.

Some interviews were fun. I don’t mind learning new things for interview. But some are just boring and systemetic. You’re trying your best to impress the interviewers.

After 1 month, I was so exhausted. I can’t believe I haven’t done anything in the whole month. I did not go to any musesums, hikes, movies in day time. I went to the gym a few times but that was it.

I had plan to go on hikes but everytime I had canceled it due to interview prepping. I just never felt like I was ready for an interview. This week, after being rejected for more than 5 companies and have no immediate interview ahead, I finally had the time to plan a small getaway trip to upstate.
