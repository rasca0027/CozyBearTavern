Lorelei and the Laser Eyes review

June 9, 2026 Kelly Chang

Good puzzles don't make you feel dumb; they make you feel clever when you solve it.

The puzzles in Lorelei and the Laser Eyes are not great. They seem like the clumsy puzzles in cheap escape rooms, you need a code for 4 digit lock? Sure! Throw in some random 4 digit number and put it next to the lock so you know it's for that specific locks. But nothing makes sense to me.

Almost all the puzzles in the game are related to years. But the lock or puzzle itself doesn't feel like it's related to years at all. Basically you just know that it's 4 digit so it must be related to years, which is stupid.

None of the puzzles make me yell out in awe. Most of them feel like brain twisters for kids, or for people who has never played any good puzzle games. It's more like pattern matching. 

They feel super repetitive too, like in the quiz club you need to answer so many random questions that's not even related to any puzzle. It feels a waste of time and I had to pause to take a break so many times.

Sure, unlocking one door gives you access to a new area, but nothing seems exciting in this game.

Overall, I don't recommend this game. If you want a better puzzle game and you don't mind a little action element in it, I highly recommend Animal Well and Tunic. The experience of playing them were full of surprises. You keep learning new things and it makes you in awe. I'm not sure if I can go back to shitty puzzle games anymore.
