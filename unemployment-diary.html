Unemployment Diary #1

June 9, 2026 Kelly Chang

Today is my 4th week since getting laid off.

I went through a lot. The first week was a mix of relief and anxiety. I was actually relieved that i don’t have to work on the bullshit ass project anymore. It was a very frustrating project where I had arguments with my stakeholders all the time. I can tell that they were stressed too. I hated my job and almost glad that I got laid off.

However, losing my job also means that I’d risk losing my work visa. I was on my last year of H1B and right before Flow filing a PERM application for my green card. The lawyers were not responsive in the past few weeks of my employment, which should have been a sign for me. I knew something was going to happen.

The second week was hopeful. I was getting lots of interviews and the companies seem great to work at. I started to have hope in working again.

Fastforward to the 4th week, it feels numbing. I lost track of everything and came down with a really bad cold. I don’t know if I want to work again, yet I was not taking the time to chill and enjoy the perfect weather. I was too stressed to do anything.
